Chapter I

Of light & shadow

01 · Light

Sun by day, moon by night

Light mode is lit by the sun, dark mode by the full moon, which sits opposite the sun: it rises at sunset, peaks at midnight and sets at dawn.

02 · Shadow

Direction, length, softness

Shadows fall away from the light. A low light casts long shadows; a high one short. Moonlight is softer and cooler than sunlight, and there's a faint lit rim on the edge facing the light.

03 · Weather

Clouds soften everything

On a clear day shadows are crisp and dark. Cloud, fog and rain spread the light across the whole sky, so shadows grow soft, short and faint and gather straight beneath things. Live conditions come from Open-Meteo for your location.

04 · Tone

Monochrome only

Ink, paper and greys, nothing else. Emphasis comes from contrast and elevation. When the light is below the horizon, only soft skylight remains.

Motion

springs · momentum · .dn-sheet
Switch

Tap it, or drag the knob and let go. It follows your finger, resists past the ends, and a flick decides where it lands.

Sheet

Opens from below and leaves the same way. Drag the handle: it tracks 1:1, rubber-bands upward, and a downward flick dismisses it.

Sky

Grab the sun or moon on the chart above and throw it along the day. It carries your momentum and settles where the flick was heading, and it resists at midnight.

Press & hover

Buttons squeeze the instant you press, not on release. Hover lifts them toward the light. Only transform and opacity animate, so shadows are never repainted mid-gesture.

Token field

web/token-field.mjs · .dn-tokens
A text box that offers something the moment a trigger character is typed, and writes what is chosen in its place.

It knows nothing about what it offers. Athena hangs it on a mechanic's rules and Fates on an objective's hint, both to name the controls Heph binds; anything with names worth remembering can use it. Arrows move, Enter or a click takes one, Escape closes.

tokenField(textarea, { items, insert: (c) => `{${c.name}}` });

Image editor

web/image-editor.mjs · .dn-crop

A picture framed before it is kept, in the shape the slot wants: drag to move it, scroll or the slider to zoom, arrows to nudge. The frame can never run off the picture — the smallest zoom is the one that just covers it — and what comes back is that rectangle, drawn at the size asked for.

const blob = await frameImage(file, { aspect: 21 / 9, edge: 2560 });

Dialogs

web/dialog.mjs · .dn-ask

The browser's own prompt() and confirm() are the one place an app stops looking like itself: Chrome's grey bar, Chrome's font, the page's hostname. These say more as well — what is about to be rewritten, listed; a complaint while the answer is wrong, with the button refused until it is right; and the destructive answer marked, with the focus left on Cancel so Enter cannot do it by accident. Escape, Cancel and the scrim all mean no.

const to = await ask({ title: 'Rename', label: 'New name', value, check });
if (await confirmAction({ title: 'Delete this?', danger: true })) remove();

Paste target

web/paste.mjs · [data-dn-paste]
Reference
Final build

Copy a picture, or take a screenshot, point at a slot and press Ctrl+V: no saving it to a file first. The one under the pointer takes it; failing that, the one with the focus; failing that, the only one on the page. With several and none pointed at, each lights up for a moment instead of one being guessed. Pasting text into a field is left alone.

pasteTarget(gallery, (files) => upload(files));

Links

web/links.mjs · a.dn-autolink

The packaging reference is at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheetos (see also www.fritolay.com).

Ctrl+click the address to open it.

Any web address in the page's text is a link, without anyone marking it as one: text written in a box shows up with its links working, including text that arrives later. A sentence's own full stop or bracket stays out of the address. Links, buttons, code and anything still being edited are left as they are. In a text box, Ctrl+click (⌘+click on a Mac) on an address opens it.

el.append(linkify(text));   // or autoLink(el) for everything under it
Chapter III

On using it

Two files. Nothing is styled until you use a .dn-* class, so it's safe to add to any page.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="diurnal.css">
<script type="module" src="diurnal.mjs"></script>

<body class="dn">                        <!-- opt the page in to the Diurnal look -->
  <button data-dn-toggle></button>      <!-- sun / moon theme switch -->
  <button class="dn-btn">Save</button>
  <footer data-dn-powered></footer>     <!-- "Powered by Diurnal UI" -->
</body>

A sheet

On materials

This panel is a translucent material: the page shows through, blurred, and its top edge catches the light. Larger surfaces read as thicker, with a stronger blur and a deeper shadow than small chips. Drag the handle down, or flick it, to put it away.
